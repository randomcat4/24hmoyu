# Post-reveal analysis

The blind judgment is complete and the synthetic text is now revealed. This is a new analytical pass; do not rewrite history to make the original guess look better.

Compare the revealed fake with matched real controls and the current style specification.

Return candidate findings in both directions:

- `PRESENCE`: behavior the fake exhibited that the real author may avoid.
- `ABSENCE`: behavior the fake lacked that the real author may characteristically exhibit.

For each finding include:

- exact local evidence in the fake
- comparable evidence from real controls when visible
- likely scope: global / register / mode / era / unknown
- whether the current style specification already covers it
- a testable validation question

Do not update the style specification in this step. Do not call a tell real merely because the fake contains it.
